Native vs Upscaled

These estimates are for native resolution — no DLSS, FSR, or XeSS. Quality mode upscaling typically adds 40–65% more frames. On laptops, upscaling is especially recommended to offset thermal constraints.

Laptop GPU Note

Laptop GPU estimates use average TGP configs. Performance varies ±20% depending on OEM cooling solution and power limit settings — some laptops downclock aggressively under sustained load.
