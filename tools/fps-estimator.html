Native vs Upscaled

These estimates are for native resolution — no DLSS, FSR, or XeSS. DLSS 4 Quality mode typically adds 40–60% more frames with minimal visual difference on supported NVIDIA GPUs.

Why 60 FPS at 1440p?

For most single-player AAA games, 60 FPS at 1440p High is the sweet spot — smooth, visually impressive, and achievable on mid-range hardware. Competitive games benefit from 144+ FPS.
