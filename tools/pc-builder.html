How allocation works

Each use case uses a different budget split. Gaming prioritizes the GPU. Content creation balances CPU and GPU. Productivity skews toward memory and storage. AI / ML loads almost everything into the GPU with at least 12 GB of VRAM.

Configure And Build

Set your budget and use case on the left, then click Build My PC to get a full component list with current pricing and direct purchase links.

Why no peripherals?

Monitor, keyboard, mouse, and headset preferences vary wildly and are easy to upgrade later. Putting the full budget into the tower means a stronger machine that won't bottleneck a future display upgrade.

Pricing notes

Listed prices are approximate 2026 USD street prices. Sales, region, and stock fluctuate — the affiliate links show live pricing at click-through. Budgets under $500 are too tight for a complete new tower.

Verify before buying

Compatibility is checked at a high level (CPU socket, RAM type, PSU wattage). Always confirm the specific motherboard supports your chosen CPU model, RAM speed, and BIOS version before placing an order.

Tower minus peripherals

A complete first-time build also needs a monitor (~$150–$400), keyboard and mouse (~$30–$150), and an OS license (~$0–$130). Plan for these separately on top of the tower cost.
