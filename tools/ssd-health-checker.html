NVMe vs SATA Temps

NVMe drives run significantly hotter than SATA SSDs — 50–60°C under load is normal. SATA SSDs should stay below 50°C. Don't compare the two directly.

What Is Drive Health %?

CrystalDiskInfo calculates drive health from SMART attributes like wear leveling, erase count, and uncorrectable error rates. Below 70% warrants serious attention.

How Long Do SSDs Last?

Most SSDs outlive their TBW (Terabytes Written) rating by a large margin. A 240GB consumer SSD rated for 80 TBW often survives 200+ TBW in real-world use.

Adding an NVMe Heatsink

A passive aluminium NVMe heatsink ($8–15) can reduce drive temps by 10–20°C. Many motherboards include one. Critical if your NVMe slot lacks airflow.
